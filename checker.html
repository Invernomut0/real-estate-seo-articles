⚖️ What We Check

The Fair Housing Act protects 7 classes. This tool scans for language that may inadvertently reference any of them:

  • Race, color, religion — religious references, neighborhood demographics tied to protected classes
  • National origin — language referencing ethnicity, accent, language preference
  • Sex/Gender — "his/her," "bachelor pad," "mom's dream"
  • Disability — references to accessibility needs in a way that implies preference
  • Family status — "perfect for families," "kid-friendly," "no kids" signs
