## Tools Reviewed (2026)

### Multiple Listing Service (MLS) AI Features
**Good for:** Data entry, basic listing creation, MLS compliance
**Not great for:** Compelling writing, marketing creativity, multi-platform distribution
**Verdict:** Your MLS is your foundation, but its built-in AI is usually basic template-filling. Use it for data, not for standout marketing.

### Property.ai / Listify / Brivity
**Good for:** Automated listing creation, basic description generation
**Not great for:** Differentiating your listings, neighborhood storytelling, compliance checking
**Verdict:** Time-savers for data-heavy listing creation. But you still spend hours tweaking descriptions to make them compelling.

**Verdict:** Powerful but requires skill. You're the AI operator, not the tool user. Great for experienced agents, overwhelming for beginners.
