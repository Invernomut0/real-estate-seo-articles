📋 The 7-Element Listing Description Framework

1. The Hook (First 1-2 Sentences)

    ✓What it is: The one or two sentences that make a buyer stop scrolling ✓Formula: [Key feature] + [Lifestyle benefit] + [Neighborhood/area] ✓Example: "Sun-drenched craftsman on tree-lined Oak Street, steps from the farmer's market and waterfront parks." — NOT "Charming home with great bones!"

2. Property Type & Key Specs

    ✓What it is: Quick identification of what the property is ✓Must include: Square footage, bed/bath count, lot size, year built ✓Style/era: Craftsman, Mid-Century Modern, Colonial, Contemporary, etc.

3. Room-by-Room Highlights (Not a Tour)

    ✓What it is: Key rooms described by their benefits, not just specs ✓Kitchen: Countertops, appliances, island, pantry — link to lifestyle (entertaining, daily cooking) ✓Living spaces: Natural light, flow, entertainment features ✓Bedrooms: Size, closet space, primary suite features ✓Outdoor: Yard, patio, deck, pool, views ✓Avoid: Listing every room like a walkthrough. Focus on what sells.

4. Neighborhood & Lifestyle Context

    ✓What it is: Why this LOCATION is desirable ✓Include: Walkability score, schools, parks, restaurants, commute times, community character ✓Crucial: Buyers often fall in love with the neighborhood first

5. Recent Updates & Investment Value

    ✓What it is: What's been improved (gives confidence to buyers) ✓List: Roof, HVAC, windows, flooring, electrical, plumbing — with approximate dates ✓ROI angle: "New kitchen ($25K) + updated baths = move-in ready, no projects needed"

6. Fair-Housing Compliance (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

    ✓Prohibited: Language referencing race, religion, sex, familial status, national origin, disability, color ✓Danger words: "perfect for kids," "ideal for retirees," "exclusive," "bohemian," "ethnic" ✓Safe language: Focus on property features, not who will live there ✓Try our free Fair Housing Description Checker before publishing

7. Call to Action

    ✓What it is: Clear next step for the buyer ✓Examples: "Schedule a private showing today," "Contact us for a broker open house," "Virtual tour link in comments" ✓Without CTA: 40%+ of readers scroll past without taking action
